# AI Interest Group: 3-Month Leadership Plan

## Vision
Make the AI IG a place where any student, even a complete beginner, can go from "what is AI?" to shipping a working project in three months, and where members learn by building together.

## Month 1: Foundations
- **Kickoff + skills survey** to learn each member's level and interests.
- **Weekly sessions:** What is AI/ML/LLMs; prompt engineering; using free AI APIs.
- **Mini task:** each member builds a "hello world" chatbot.
- **Setup:** shared GitHub org, WhatsApp/Discord group, and a pinned resource list.

## Month 2: Build
- **Workshops:** build a chatbot with Gradio; intro to RAG; deploying on Hugging Face Spaces.
- **Projects:** form teams of 3-4, each picking a small project (study assistant, notes Q&A bot, image classifier).
- **Weekly check-ins** to unblock teams and review code.

## Month 3: Showcase
- **Mini-hackathon** (one day) to finish and polish projects.
- **Demo day:** teams present to the µLearn community.
- **Wrap-up:** publish projects and a how-to guide, collect feedback, and prepare handover notes for the next lead.

## Engagement and mentoring
- **Peer pairing:** match beginners with experienced members.
- **Weekly office hours** for questions and debugging.
- **Recognition:** shout-outs for contributions and a "project of the month."
- **Inclusive by default:** beginner-friendly tasks, recorded sessions and notes for anyone who misses a meeting.

## Success measures
Attendance, number of projects completed, number of members who deploy something, and member feedback.
